# PC Chairs Rebuttal Format Email, 2026-05-30

Source: user-provided latest email from ACM MM 2026 PC Chairs. Treat this as the highest-priority operational rule unless a newer official PC/ACM MM/OpenReview instruction supersedes it.

## Binding Rules From The Email

1. Each paper may include only **one single-page PDF** containing all responses to reviewer comments.
2. The one-page limit is **strict**. Rebuttals exceeding one page will not be considered.
3. There is no strict formatting template, but the PDF must use a reasonable layout with legible font size and clear line spacing.
4. The one-page PDF must include all rebuttal content, including any references or supporting material.
5. Do **not** use the `Official Comment` feature to reply individually to reviewers.
6. Official Comments are not visible to reviewers and will not be considered for rebuttal.
7. The one-page rebuttal PDF will be visible to reviewers, AC, SAC, and PCs.
8. Rebuttal submission is optional.
9. Authors may focus only on reviewer comments requiring clarification or response.
10. The rebuttal must preserve anonymity.
11. Publicly available work or established facts may be cited, but references must not be used to move substantive rebuttal content outside the one-page limit.

## Operational Consequence For CHORD

- The final upload target is only `author_response_upload_target_expected_*.pdf` or its final real-result successor, after it is verified to be exactly one page.
- The five-page `author_response_min_diff_expected_YYYYMMDD_HHMM.pdf` remains an internal scientific master only and must not be uploaded.
- Do not submit per-reviewer Official Comments.
- Do not rely on any extra attachments, external links, old supplementary material, or comment threads for scientific rebuttal evidence.
- All reviewer-facing claims, tables, figure snippets, definitions, references, and commitments must fit inside the single one-page PDF.
